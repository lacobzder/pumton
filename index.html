The Order of Service

The congregation will remain standing for as long as the crown does.

  1. 1 The Entrance

    Pumton does not walk in. He is placed, already sitting, already holding the sceptre.

  2. 2 The Recognition

    The congregation is asked whether it recognises him.

    All shall say: it is a dog.

  3. 3 The Oath

    He is asked to promise things. He holds the sceptre a little tighter. This is accepted as an answer.

  4. 4 The Anointing

    Traditionally oil. Here, one green and white sceptre the size of his whole arm, which he was holding before anyone arrived.

  5. 5 The Crowning

    The crown is set on his head by the one in the knitted hat. It does not stay on by itself.

    Someone must keep holding it. That someone is you.

  6. 6 The Homage

    Each person present holds the crown for as long as they can. Every hold makes a new king, numbered in order.

  7. 7 The Recess

    The crown falls. It is picked up off the floor.

    The service resumes at 5.
