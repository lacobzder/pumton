# PUMTON . the court circular

form: the daily court circular, the small print column where a palace lists who held what and for how long. pumton is the king. the kingmaker (pink knitted hat) holds the crown on. the crown falls a lot. every entry is dry, dated, and slightly too precise.

---

## for the bio (pick one)

- king for exactly as long as someone is holding the crown on
- the crown does not stay on by itself. please hold
- pumton. sceptre in both paws since before anyone arrived
- currently reigning: depends who is holding
- court circular of a very small kingdom. everything he can see from where he sits

---

## the circular (posts)

**1**
the palace, tuesday.
pumton this morning held the sceptre.
the crown was held on by the kingmaker for 4.21 seconds, after which it was on the floor.
pumton xii, the hesitant, is remembered fondly.

**2**
the kingmaker has asked us to confirm he does not want the crown.
he wants to hold it on.
we have confirmed this.

**3**
corrections: yesterday's circular described pumton iv as "the steady". he reigned 0.4 seconds. he was the blink. we regret the error

**4**
the palace, thursday.
pumton received nobody, as is customary.
the sceptre was not put down.

**5**
someone held the crown on for eleven minutes last night. we have checked and we do not have an epithet for that. pumton cccxi, the unmoving. we made one up

**6**
the green and white sceptre is about the length of his whole arm. it has never been asked about. it will not be

**7** (meme caption, the banner image)
him: holding the sceptre
the one in the hat: holding the crown on
you: holding

**c.** small dog. paste crown. a sceptre he will not discuss. a friend in a pink hat who keeps the crown on with one paw. that is the whole kingdom. $PUMTON, link in reply

**d.** every hold of the crown is a reign. every reign goes in the roll of kings. we have had pumton the brief, pumton the hesitant and one pumton the great. link in reply
